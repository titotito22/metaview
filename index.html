Get started in 5 steps

You need Ray-Ban Meta glasses, the Meta AI app and an AI key.

  1. Install the Meta AI app and pair your glasses.
  2. In Meta AI turn on Developer Mode: App Settings → App Info → tap App version 5 times.
  3. Install MetaView (Android or iPhone below) and tap Connect.
  4. Settings → choose OpenAI, Gemini or Grok → paste your API key.
  5. Tap the mic, or arm the wake word and say “Hey Jarvis”.
