## What it does

Say **"Hey Jarvis"** (or any name you choose) and talk. MetaView sees through
your glasses' camera and answers in your ear.

| | |
|---|---|
| 👁️ **Look & describe** | "What am I looking at?" describes the scene, identifies objects, plants, landmarks and products |
| 📖 **Read & summarize** | Reads signs, menus and documents aloud, or sums them up |
| 🌍 **Live translation** | Listens to the people around you and says the translation in your language, or works as a two-way interpreter |
| ♟️ **Chess coach** | Watches your real chess board live and tells you the best next move |
| 🔳 **QR & barcode scan** | Scan with your glasses; links open with one tap |
| 🧠 **Memory** | "Remember I parked on level 3" … "Where did I park?" |
| 🏃 **Fitness tracker** | Walking & cycling with GPS, pace, steps and spoken coaching. Ask *"How far have I cycled?"* |
| 🧭 **Navigation** | "Take me to the nearest pharmacy" opens Google Maps directions |
| 🎥 **Photos & video** | Capture from the glasses in portrait or landscape |
| 📡 **Live streaming** | Stream your point of view to YouTube, Twitch, Kick or OBS |
| 🎙️ **Offline wake word** | Hands-free, works with the screen locked, no internet needed |
| 🌙 **Dark mode** | Plus English & Arabic speech support |

Works with **OpenAI, Google Gemini or xAI Grok**: use your own API key,
stored only on your phone.

## Getting started

1. Install the **Meta AI** app and pair your glasses.
2. In Meta AI, turn on **Developer Mode**:
   App Settings → App Info → tap *App version* 5 times → Developer Mode on.
3. Open MetaView → **Connect** → approve in Meta AI.
4. Settings → choose **OpenAI / Gemini / Grok** and paste your API key.
5. Tap the mic, or arm the wake word and say **"Hey Jarvis"**.

## Requirements

- Ray-Ban Meta glasses + the Meta AI app
- Android 12+ (64-bit) or iPhone with iOS 16+
- An OpenAI, Gemini or Grok API key

## Privacy

- Your API keys stay on your phone.
- Voice and images go only to the AI provider **you** choose.
- The wake word runs fully offline on your phone.
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
